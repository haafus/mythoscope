57 · Contagion — simple vs complex spreading as a generative model

Epidemiology of representations, as a model-comparison. On a small-world tradition network (k-NN geography + weak long-range ties), a motif spreads under two rules: simple contagion (one exposure — crosses weak ties, can jump) vs complex contagion (needs a fraction of neighbours — stalls at weak ties, stays compact). We compare each real motif's geographic fragmentation at its size to both simulated ensembles and assign the better-fitting mechanism. No time axis → this is model-comparison on the snapshot, not history; the network is a geographic proxy.
