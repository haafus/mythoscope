47 · Admixture

The proper population-genetics admixture model fit to the motif matrix: X[t,m] ~ Bernoulli(Q·P), where each tradition's row of Q is its ancestry proportions over K latent motif-pools and P is each pool's motif frequencies (exact EM). K is chosen by cross-validation, exactly as ADMIXTURE does — and the CV curve plateaus with no sharp minimum, the diagnostic that the material is clinal (a continuum of mixtures, not K discrete populations). A principled, dated replacement for mockup 45's ad-hoc k=6 NMF.
