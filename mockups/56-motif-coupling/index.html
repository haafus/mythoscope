56 · Motif coupling — pairwise maximum-entropy (inverse-Ising)

The minimal model reproducing the observed pairwise co-occurrences is the Ising model P(s) ∝ exp(Σ h·s + Σ J·s·s). Its couplings Jij are the direct links — what survives after every transitive path through other motifs is removed. Fit by L1-logistic pseudo-likelihood over the top-500 motifs, effort-corrected (log-richness covariate). Positive J = direct attraction; negative J = mutual exclusion (invisible to the marginals); high correlation with J ≈ 0 = an indirect pair.
