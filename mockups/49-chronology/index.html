49 · Chronology

Can we put a time axis on the motifs? Three linked views (one build), per dating-and-chronology-methods: ① Datability — which dating route each motif admits; ② Barrier floors — absolute year lower-bounds from biogeographic barriers; ③ Pseudo-chronology — a consensus relative order, rooted by the barriers, with an honest map of where it is unreliable.
