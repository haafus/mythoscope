48 · Teleconnections

The climate-science idea of a teleconnection — a correlation between distant places that survives after the local (distance) trend is removed — applied to myth. Edges link macro-areas that share motifs far more than their >3000 km separation predicts (the reticulation isolation-by-distance leaves unexplained); communities are clustered from that teleconnection matrix; and the teleconnector motifs — the disjunct, near-global substrate responsible — are surfaced and dated by M17.
