45 · Stratigraphic peeling

Recursive coverage-corrected peeling of the Berezkin matrix, in two spaces. Geography (motif-attestation): a hard peel + two soft-factor models (NMF, effort-corrected M38 Poisson) — but the structure is clinal (isolation-by-distance, weak silhouettes), so its "layers" are a discretisation of a gradient. Worldview (theme-profile, the mockup 16/23 axis): the same peel is far more modular (higher silhouettes) — real strata running young märchen/trickster → deep cosmology, and crossing continents. Everything is dated by the M17 depth score. The finding: the two axes differ in topology — geography is a continuum, worldview has discrete layers.
