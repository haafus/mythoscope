58 · Higher-order structure — synergy triples & the topology of trait-space

Two things the pairwise view (mockups 53 / 56) cannot see. Synergy: motif triples that form a package only together — measured by interaction information II(X;Y;Z) = ΣH(pairs) − ΣH(singles) − H(triple), where >0 means the triple carries dependency beyond every one of its pairs. Topology: the shape of the motif cloud (each of the top-300 motifs a point in 948-dim tradition-incidence space, Jaccard distance) via persistent homology — H0 counts connected components, H1 counts loops, across every scale. The two together ask: is trait-space lumpy and cyclic, or one connected blob?

The topological verdict

The best candidates in the whole catalogue still have II < 0 — even the least-redundant triple is redundant. No motif package exceeds the sum of its pairs.

Most redundant triples — lowest II

The floor is variant-families: a motif, its subtype, and a sub-subtype share almost all their information — a single fact recorded thrice.
