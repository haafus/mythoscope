52 · Diversity turnover

Ecology's diversity split applied to motifs by macro-area. Alpha (per-tradition richness) just tracks cataloguing effort — over-studied Europe on top — so it is not a diversity signal. Beta (turnover = γ/α) is far less effort-driven and is: low beta = a homogeneous shared stock (a diffusion belt), high beta = internally divergent traditions.
