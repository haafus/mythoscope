The tradition × motif matrix read as a landscape-genetics dataset. Isolation-by-distance (Mantel) and an AMOVA / Fst variance-partition quantify how areal the material is; an EEMS-lite effective-migration surface maps where motif similarity decays faster than distance predicts (barriers) or slower (corridors), with the strongest long-range bridges drawn as edges. A descriptive re-encoding — cultural transmission is horizontal and biased, so the drift null is analogical, not demographic.
