55 · Ancestral repertoires

Set-level ancestral-state reconstruction: a motif is placed at a language family's proto-node if it is attested in ≥ 2 of the family's primary sub-branches (parsimony — present in two sister clades ⇒ present in their ancestor). Aggregated per family, this is a reconstructed proto-mythology, dated by the family's expansion age (M30). Coarse (family-resolution, presence-only), oldest family first.

Chips show each motif and how many sub-branches attest it (of the family's total) — more branches = more securely ancestral. This dates the *inherited* repertoire only; the deep pan-global substrate (sun & moon, primeval waters) shows up in every family independently, which is why it can't be pinned to any one proto-node.
