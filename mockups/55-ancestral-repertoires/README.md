# 55 · Ancestral repertoires

Set-level **ancestral-state reconstruction** — reconstructed proto-mythologies (analysis #9).

A motif is placed at a language family's **proto-node** if attested in **≥ 2 of the family's primary sub-branches**
(parsimony: present in two sister clades ⇒ present in their ancestor). Aggregated per family this is a
**reconstructed proto-repertoire**, dated by the family's expansion age (M30's table). Nine families, oldest first
— proto-Afro-Asiatic (~7000 BCE) → proto-Turkic (~200 BCE).

**Finding.** The reconstructions are legible: **proto-Austronesian** (~3200 BCE) recovers a celestial/cosmogonic
core (*Male sun & female moon, Venus, Rainbow serpent, Milky-Way serpent*); **proto-Indo-European** (~3500 BCE)
the märchen core (*Magic wife 21/22, A man in search of the woman, The dragon-slayer*); **proto-Afro-Asiatic** the
oldest layer (*Waters split apart, Trickster-hare*). **Honest caveat, visible on the page:** the deep pan-global
substrate (*Male sun & female moon, Primeval waters, Figure on lunar disc*) reconstructs in **every** family
independently — which is exactly why it can't be pinned to any one proto-node (it predates them all, or is
reinvented — mockup 51's undecidability).

`build_data.py` groups traditions by Glottolog family and primary sub-branch, reconstructs by the ≥2-branch rule,
and dates by M30's family table; writes `data.js`.
