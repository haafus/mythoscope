60 · Worldview peel across catalogues — the same stratigraphic peel in each catalogue's own taxonomy

The worldview half of mockup 45, ported to all three indexes so they are directly comparable. Each catalogue's units (Berezkin traditions · ATU peoples · TMI cultures) are recursively peeled by their profile over that catalogue's native categories (CLR, coverage-aware). A node's name = a depth register (Deep / Young, from the share of the catalogue's archaic/mythic categories) + its signature category; the depth index is the mean cross-continent breadth of the categories it emphasises (broad = old). The continent bar is on every node on purpose — ATU and TMI are Euro-/literary-biased, so their "deep" layer is partly the over-catalogued European core, not antiquity.
