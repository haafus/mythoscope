Zeus

Youngest son of Kronos and Rhea; king of the gods of Olympos. He overthrows the Titans, divides the cosmos with Poseidon and Hades, and reigns as god of sky, weather, law, and fate.

Roles
Sky, Thunder, Kingship, Justice
Epithets
Cloud-gatherer, Aegis-bearing, Father of gods and men
Actions
Overthrows Kronos; swallows Metis; fathers Athena, Apollo, Artemis, Ares
