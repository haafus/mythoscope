# Research context

Background surveys of the field Mythoscope sits inside — the computational study
of folk narrative and myth. These are *landscape and method* docs, distinct from
the source-pipeline documentation under [`../motifs/`](../motifs/).

- [`computational-folkloristics-survey.md`](computational-folkloristics-survey.md) — field survey (classical 2008–2018 and modern 2018–2026 eras): indices, topic models, embeddings, phylogenetics, networks, Propp extraction — with datasets, licensing and recommendations
- [`computational-folkloristics-landscape.md`](computational-folkloristics-landscape.md) — labs, people, journals, conferences and landmark publications
- [`motif-induction-review.md`](motif-induction-review.md) — methods and algorithms for inducing motifs from text (practitioner review)
- [`motif-induction-survey.md`](motif-induction-survey.md) — survey of motif-induction research, 2018–2026
- [`dating-and-chronology-methods.md`](dating-and-chronology-methods.md) — how to put a time axis on the motif corpus: a ranked catalogue of absolute-dating methods (biogeographic bounds, language/genetic calibration, tip-dating, …) and the more tractable relative-ordering route (CA seriation, single-cell pseudotime, NeighborNet, nestedness) with polarity/rooting and validation — grounded in d'Huy / Tehrani / Thuillard–Berezkin and method transfer from genetics, archaeology and single-cell biology
- [`mythology-encyclopedias-survey.md`](mythology-encyclopedias-survey.md) — how the major reference works (encyclopedias, handbooks, atlases) carve the mythological world into regions and traditions
- [`corpus-sourcing-survey.md`](corpus-sourcing-survey.md) — what full text is actually obtainable per tradition: sources, editions, licensing and gaps for the embedding corpus
