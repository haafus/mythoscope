# Research context

Background surveys of the research field MythoScope sits inside — the study of
folk narrative and myth by computational methods. These are *landscape and
method* docs, distinct from the source-pipeline documentation under
[`../motifs/`](../motifs/).

| [`computational-folkloristics-landscape.md`](computational-folkloristics-landscape.md) | Labs, people, journals, conferences and landmark publications in computational folkloristics & computational mythology |
